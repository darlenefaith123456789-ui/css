Credit Scoring Decision Support System

Decide on installment loan applications faster and more consistently.

Enter an applicant's details and the loan they are asking for. The system checks them against what it learned from past financing records and tells you how risky the applicant is, what it recommends, and why.

Assess an applicant

What the result means

  • Low risk Likely to pay on time. Approve
  • Medium risk Some warning signs. Look closer. Review
  • High risk Similar to past borrowers who defaulted. Reject

How to use it

  1. 1

    Enter the application

    Fill in the applicant’s personal details, income, existing debt, the loan amount and term, and their past repayment record.

  2. 2

    Read the recommendation

    The system shows the risk level, a credit score out of 100, and the main reasons that raised or lowered the risk.

  3. 3

    Make the decision

    Use the recommendation together with your own judgment. Every assessment is saved so it can be reviewed later.

What the system looks at

  • Applicant

    Age, marital status and occupation

  • Ability to pay

    Monthly income, existing debt, and how much of the income the installment takes

  • The loan

    Amount, term, interest rate and how interest is computed

  • Payment history

    Missed payments and late payment penalties on past loans

Good to know

  • The system supports the loan officer. The final decision is always yours.
  • Results are only as good as the information entered, so check the details before assessing.
  • The same applicant details always give the same result, so every applicant is judged the same way.
